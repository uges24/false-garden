# Water Sky Target Analysis

Locked target: `docs/visual-targets/water-sky/target-reference-v1.png`

## Camera Height

The camera is extremely low, roughly at grass height, looking slightly upward. Foreground grass blades occupy the bottom edge and partially occlude the marble.

## Horizon Line

The horizon and ground plane sit around the lower third of the frame, approximately 62-68% down from the top. Distant hills and ruin silhouettes overlap the misty horizon.

## Sun Position

The sun is near the upper center, slightly above the vertical midpoint of the water ceiling. It is warm gold/pearl, not pure white, and it blooms through water distortion.

## Marble Position

The marble is centered horizontally in the lower foreground, around 78-82% down from the top. It is small relative to the frame, reflective cyan/blue, and used as the primary scale anchor.

## Grass Density

Grass is dense across the lower 25-30% of the frame. The foreground is lush emerald with individual blades and bright yellow/cyan glints.

## Ruins / Rocks Placement

Tall ruin pillars frame the scene on the left and right thirds, with smaller central/distant pillars. Rocks and mountain-like forms sit along the horizon, especially right of center.

## Water Ceiling Coverage

The water ceiling dominates roughly the upper 60-70% of the image. It curves visually through perspective and feels like an overhead ocean surface, not a flat panel.

## Caustic Density

Caustics are dense and high contrast near the sun and throughout the water, with bright broken linework. They are varied in thickness and intensity, denser near bright regions.

## Beam Direction

Light beams originate near the sun and descend diagonally/vertically toward the grass and marble area. They widen and soften toward the ground.

## Particle Density

Particles are visible in beams and mist, especially around the center and foreground. They are numerous but not evenly distributed; they cluster in light paths.

## Color Palette

Dominant colors: deep teal, cyan, emerald green, warm gold, pearl white, dark blue-green shadows. The target has strong warm/cool contrast.

## Contrast / Depth

High contrast: dark foreground and side silhouettes, bright caustics and sun, misty midground, and deep teal water overhead. Depth is created through layered grass, ruins, mist, beams, particles, and the water ceiling.
